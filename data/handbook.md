# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Version 3.2. Applies to all full-time employees.

## 1. Working Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees may schedule the remaining hours flexibly, as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Overtime must be approved by a manager in advance. Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu, taken within 60 days.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. This increases to 25 days after five years of service. Leave accrues monthly, so an employee who joins mid-year receives a prorated amount.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days require two weeks of notice.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce it.

## 4. Public Holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The full list is published on the intranet every December. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is not moved to a weekday.

## 5. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave must begin within 6 months of the birth or adoption. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks.

## 6. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a department head and are reviewed every 6 months. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours. A home office stipend of 500 USD is available once, within the first 90 days of employment.

## 7. Travel Reimbursement

Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. For flights over 6 hours, premium economy is allowed. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night in standard cities and 250 USD per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London, and Tokyo.

## 8. Meal Allowance

During business travel, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 60 USD. This covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Meals with clients can be claimed separately with an itemized receipt and the names of attendees.

## 9. Expense Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Claims over 100 USD require a receipt. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless a manager approves an exception in writing. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 10. Laptop and Equipment Policy

Every new employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. Personal devices may not be used to access production systems.

## 11. Password and Security Rules

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Passwords are rotated only after a suspected breach, not on a fixed schedule. Employees must never share credentials, including with colleagues or IT staff.

## 12. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are grounds for immediate disciplinary action. Concerns can be reported to a manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.

## 13. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers goals set in the previous cycle and new goals for the next one. Employees receive written feedback from their manager and may add their own comments.

## 14. Learning Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is covered separately under the travel policy.

## 15. Contractors

Contractors receive 10 days of paid annual leave per contract year. This does not increase with length of service, and unused days cannot be carried over. Contractors do not receive paid sick leave; sick days are unpaid and must be reported to the contractor's manager on the same day. Contractors are not eligible for the learning budget or the home office stipend. Contractors receive a company laptop, which must be returned on the last day of the contract.

## 16. Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive annual leave and sick leave in proportion to their contracted days. For example, an employee contracted for 3 days per week receives 12 days of annual leave and 6 paid sick days per year. All other leave rules, including carry-over and the doctor's note requirement, are the same as for full-time employees.

## 17. Berlin Office

Employees employed by the Berlin office receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, which does not increase with length of service. Instead of the company list, the Berlin office observes the public holidays of the state of Berlin. Sick leave rules are the same as for other employees, except that a doctor's note is required from the third consecutive day of absence.

## 18. Forms and Systems

Leave of any kind is requested in Atlas, the HR portal. Annual leave requests longer than 5 consecutive days use form HR-17, which must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Parental leave is announced with form HR-22, at least 8 weeks before the leave starts. Business travel is booked in Compass, the company travel portal. Expense claims are submitted in Ledger using form FIN-04. Lost or stolen equipment is reported to IT with form IT-09.
